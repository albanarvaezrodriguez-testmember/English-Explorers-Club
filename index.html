Podcast Post #01

Tune In and Learn: BBC 6 Minute English

What is it?

BBC 6 Minute English is a free podcast produced by the BBC Learning English team. Each episode lasts approximately six minutes and covers everyday topics such as technology, nature, sport, and culture. The language used is clear and natural, making it ideal for upper primary learners aged 10 to 12.

Educational Value

This resource helps students develop their listening comprehension skills while expanding vocabulary in context. Each episode includes a quiz question at the beginning, encouraging students to make predictions and engage actively with the content. Transcripts are also available, which allows teachers to use the podcast for combined listening and reading activities.

How to Use it in Class

Teachers can play one episode per week as a warm-up activity. Students listen once for general understanding and a second time for specific details. Afterwards, a short class discussion or written response can consolidate learning.

Benefits and Challenges

One of the main benefits of this resource is its accessibility — it is completely free and available on the BBC website and major podcast platforms. However, some episodes may include advanced vocabulary, so teachers should preview each one before using it with their students.

Role-Playing Activity Post #02

Speak Up! Role-Playing Activities for the EFL Classroom

What is it?

This resource, provided by the British Council's Teaching English platform, offers a collection of ready-to-use role-playing activities designed for English language learners. The activities present real-life communicative scenarios such as shopping, asking for directions, making plans with friends, or visiting a doctor.

Educational Value

Role-playing encourages students to use English in meaningful, authentic contexts. It develops speaking and listening skills simultaneously, while also building students' confidence when communicating in a foreign language. For 5th and 6th grade learners, this type of activity bridges the gap between classroom language and real-world communication.

How to Use it in Class

Teachers can assign roles to pairs or small groups and give students a few minutes to prepare before performing their dialogue. The activity works well as a speaking assessment or as a fun end-of-unit task. Scripts and prompts are provided, so students at different levels can participate comfortably.

Benefits and Challenges

Role-playing is highly motivating for this age group and promotes collaborative learning. A possible challenge is that some students may feel shy or reluctant to speak in front of their peers. Creating a supportive and non-judgmental classroom atmosphere is essential for this activity to be successful.

Picture Dictionary Post #03

A Picture is Worth a Thousand Words: Visual Dictionary Online

What is it?

Visual Dictionary Online is a free interactive resource that organises vocabulary thematically through detailed, labelled illustrations. Topics include the human body, nature, science, transport, food, and many more. Each image is accompanied by the corresponding English word, making it an excellent tool for building and reinforcing vocabulary.

Educational Value

Visual dictionaries are particularly effective for learners in the 10–12 age range, as they connect new words to clear visual representations, which aids memorisation and retention. This resource supports vocabulary learning across multiple subject areas, making it a versatile tool for Content and Language Integrated Learning (CLIL) contexts as well.

How to Use it in Class

Teachers can use this dictionary to introduce new vocabulary before a reading or writing activity. Students can also explore it independently to look up unknown words. A suggested activity is to ask students to choose a theme, explore the related vocabulary, and then write a short descriptive paragraph using at least eight new words.

Benefits and Challenges

The main advantage of this resource is its visual and intuitive design, which makes it engaging and easy to navigate. One limitation is that it does not include audio pronunciation, so teachers may need to supplement it with a pronunciation tool or model the words themselves.

Digital Storytelling Post #04

Once Upon a Digital Time: Storytelling with Storybird

What is it?

Storybird is an online creative writing platform that allows children to read, write, and illustrate their own stories using a library of professional artwork. The platform offers a wide variety of genres and visual styles, giving students the freedom to create personalised narratives in English.

Educational Value

Digital storytelling develops a broad range of language skills, including reading, writing, vocabulary, and narrative structure. By creating their own stories, students engage in meaningful and creative language production. Storybird also fosters a sense of authorship and pride in their work, which significantly increases motivation.

How to Use it in Class

As a guided activity, teachers can ask students to write a short story of at least five sentences using a specific set of vocabulary or grammar structures studied in class. Stories can then be shared with classmates, turning the activity into a peer-reading exercise. Teachers can also use Storybird to create model stories and share them with the class as reading comprehension activities.

Benefits and Challenges

Storybird is visually appealing and highly intuitive for this age group. The high-quality illustrations inspire even reluctant writers to engage with the task. A potential challenge is that the full range of features requires a paid subscription, although the free version is sufficient for basic storytelling activities.

Extra Resource 1 Post #05

Game On! Interactive Quizzes with Kahoot!

What is it?

Kahoot! is a game-based learning platform that allows teachers to create interactive quizzes, surveys, and challenges. Students participate using any device with internet access, answering questions in real time while competing against their classmates in a fun and dynamic environment.

Educational Value

Kahoot! is an excellent tool for reviewing and consolidating grammar and vocabulary in a low-pressure, enjoyable context. The competitive but friendly format keeps students engaged and motivated, and the immediate feedback helps both teachers and learners identify areas that need further practice.

How to Use it in Class

Teachers can create customised quizzes aligned with the current unit — for example, a quiz on irregular verbs, question forms, or topic-specific vocabulary. Kahoot! can be used at the end of a lesson as a review activity or before a test as a revision tool. There are also thousands of ready-made EFL quizzes available in the Kahoot! library.

Benefits and Challenges

The main strength of this resource is its ability to transform a traditional grammar or vocabulary review into an exciting classroom event. Students are consistently enthusiastic when Kahoot! is used. One challenge is that it requires a stable internet connection and at least one device per student or pair, which may not always be available in every school context.

Extra Resource 2 Post #06

Learn English Through Music: LyricsTraining

What is it?

LyricsTraining is an online platform that helps language learners improve their listening and writing skills through music. Students watch official music videos and complete the lyrics by typing missing words as they hear them. The platform offers different difficulty levels, from beginner to expert, making it adaptable to a wide range of learner profiles.

Educational Value

Music is a powerful tool for language learning. It exposes students to natural pronunciation, rhythm, and intonation while making vocabulary and grammar acquisition more memorable. LyricsTraining combines listening, reading, and writing skills in a single activity, offering a genuinely integrated language learning experience.

How to Use it in Class

Teachers can select a song related to the current topic or simply one that students enjoy, and assign it as a listening gap-fill activity. After completing the activity, the class can discuss the meaning of the song, identify new vocabulary, or even write a short response. This works particularly well as a Friday activity or as a reward at the end of a productive week.

Benefits and Challenges

LyricsTraining is free, visually engaging, and highly motivating for learners aged 10 to 12, who often have strong connections to popular music. One consideration is that teachers should preview song content carefully to ensure it is age-appropriate before using it in class.
